# Documentation & Training Consultancy: Capability Statement

Senior documentation and training specialists who build the systems, pipelines, and content that make technical knowledge usable.

---

## Who We Are

We are a senior-level documentation and training consultancy founded by two practitioners with **nearly 40 years of combined experience** across the full spectrum of the technology industry. From global enterprises (HP, Oracle, Red Hat, Bentley Systems) to agile startups (Stylelabs), we have operated at every level of the stack.

We are **not "just" technical writers**. We are engineers, architects, and operators who write. Our unique value proposition is the ability to **design, build, test, and deliver** the entire documentation ecosystem—from the underlying code and CI/CD pipelines to the final published content and enterprise-wide training programs.

---

## Our Core Philosophy

Modern documentation is not a static artifact; it is a **software product**. It requires version control, automated testing, continuous integration, and rigorous quality assurance. We bring the discipline of software engineering to the craft of communication.

*   **Full-Stack Documentation:** We don't just write the manual; we build the pipeline that generates it.
*   **Technical Depth:** We understand the systems we document because we have administered them.
*   **Scalable Solutions:** We design workflows that grow with your organization, from solo developers to global enterprises.

*   **Standards & Governance:** Establishing style guides, structured authoring standards, and cross-product alignment for distributed teams.

### 3. Enterprise Training & Enablement

We deliver high-impact training programs using proven pedagogical frameworks:

*   **Methodology:** **Train-the-Trainer** models, and the **Tell-Show-Do-Apply** cycle to ensure retention and adoption.
*   **Delivery:** Extensive experience in both **virtual and in-person** settings for global audiences.
*   **Materials:** Creating comprehensive workbooks, lesson plans, video tutorials (OBS, OpenShot), and interactive learning paths.

### 4. Technology Agnosticism & Rapid Adaptation

We possess deep, practical knowledge across nearly every major technology stack.

*   **Breadth:** From legacy mainframes to modern cloud-native architectures.
