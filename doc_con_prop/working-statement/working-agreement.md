## 1. Core Principle: Leadership & Authority
This partnership is founded on the principle that **MFH is the Strategic Lead** and **AA is the Operational Executor**.

*   **MFH's Domain (Final Say):**
    *   **Strategy & Vision:** Defining the service offering, methodology, and long-term direction.
    *   **Quality Standards:** Final approval on all documentation, training materials, and deliverables before client submission.
    *   **Client Strategy:** Deciding how to approach clients, pricing strategy, and negotiation terms.
    *   **Team Direction:** Setting the pace, priorities, and workload distribution.
    *   **Technology:** Defining team overall tooling. 
*   **AA's Domain (Final Say):**
    *   **Implementation:** Selecting the specific tools, pipelines, and technical architecture to execute MFH's strategy.
    *   **Operations:** Administrative logistics.
    *   **Feasibility:** Advising on technical constraints, but deferring to MFH's strategic priority if a trade-off is required.
*   **Joint Decisions:**
    *   Taking on new clients or pilots.
    *   Major financial commitments (e.g., purchasing software licenses).
    *   Changes to the partnership structure itself.
    *   **Implementation:** Selecting the specific tools, pipelines, and technical architecture to execute MFH's strategy.
    *   **Operations:** Managing the repository and CI/CD.

> **Authority Clause:** In any area of disagreement where a decision is blocked, **MFH's strategic judgment prevails**. AA's role is to ensure the execution matches that judgment, not to override it.
>
> **Draft Status:** This document is a **proposal for discussion only**. MFH has full authority to rewrite, delete, or amend any section of this agreement at any time. The goal is to capture *her* vision of how we work, not to constrain it with pre-written rules.

## 2. Operating Rhythm (Async-First)
Given our time zone differences (approx. 8 hours) and full-time employment:

*   **Default Mode:** Asynchronous communication via email/Slack/GitHub Issues. We do not expect immediate responses.
*   **Overlap Window:** We aim for a 4-hour daily overlap for synchronous syncs if needed.
*   **Meeting Cadence:**
    *   **Weekly Sync:** 30 mins (Video/Audio). Focus: Strategic alignment, blockers, and review of MFH's direction.
    *   **Ad-Hoc:** Unplanned calls only for urgent fires (rare).
*   **Communication Protocol:**
    *   **No "Urgent" unless critical.** We respect each other's priMFH jobs.
    *   **Decisions:** Documented in the repo (README or Issue) rather than lost in chat.

## 3. Workflow & Implementation (Shared Execution)
*   **The "No-BS" Standard:** Both partners commit to direct, honest feedback. We critique the work, not the person.
*   **Collaborative Build:** While MFH sets the *standard* and *direction*, **both partners will implement**.
    *   **MFH:** Leads on editorial strategy, content modeling, and quality assurance. She may also execute specific high-level tasks or complex modeling.
    *   **AA:** Leads on pipeline architecture, tooling, and automation. He may also execute specific writing tasks if directed.
    *   **Handover:** Tasks are assigned based on capacity and expertise, not rigid roles. Both partners are expected to be hands-on with the work.
*   **Review Process:**
    1.  Work is produced (by either partner) based on agreed specs.
    2.  MFH reviews for strategic alignment and quality.
    3.  If MFH rejects a deliverable, she provides the *direction* for the fix; the assignee executes the fix.
    4.  **Final Sign-off:** Only MFH signs off on client-facing work.
*   **Tooling:** Git-based workflow (Docs-as-Code). All work tracked in issues/PRs.

## 4. Growth & Development
*   **Skill Sharing:** We commit to sharing knowledge. 
*   **Professional Branding:** We present a unified front. Publicly, we speak as one voice. Internally, we debate openly.

## 5. Exit & Conflict Resolution
*   **Conflict:** If we reach an impasse, we pause. If unresolved after 48 hours, we revert to MFH's strategic direction (as per Section 1).
*   **Exit:** Either partner may dissolve the venture with 30 days' notice.
    *   **IP:** Work created jointly belongs to the partnership until dissolved. Upon dissolution, IP splits 50/50 or is assigned to the continuing partner.
    *   **Clients:** Active clients are handed over to the continuing partner; no poaching for 12 months.

## 6. Our Commitment to Each Other
*   **To MFH:** AA commits to providing the operational backbone that allows MFH to lead without administrative friction.
*   **To AA:** MFH commits to providing clear strategic direction and trusting AA's execution.
*   **To the Venture:** We commit to honesty, reliability, and the belief that our combined skills are greater than the sum of our parts.
