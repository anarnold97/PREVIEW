### Proposed Agenda: Consultancy Pilot & Logistics

**Objective:** Align on operating model, geography, pilot candidates, and the constraints of running this business model alongside full-time employment.




**1. Geographic & Operational Reality**

- Ireland is 8 hours ahead of Seattle.
- Live overlap window: **9:00 AM–1:00 PM Seattle / 5:00 PM–9:00 PM Ireland.**
- Everything outside that window has to be asynchronous — GitHub, docs, threaded messages.
- **Questions:**
    - Is a 4-hour live window per day workable, or do we need to be stricter about async?
    - How do we handle client-facing responsiveness if a client expects same-day replies during US business hours?
    - Do we set fixed "office hours" within the overlap, or meet ad hoc?
    - What commitments are we expected to make over the weekend?
    - Do we do front desk duties?




**2. The Full-Time Job Constraint**

Both parties are expected to remain employed full-time for the foreseeable future. 

This isn't a side detail — it shapes what the business can realistically be.

- **Capacity:** Realistically, how many hours per week can each person commit without damaging their day job?
- **Conflict of interest:** Does either employer have clauses restricting outside consulting, particularly in adjacent industries?
- **Sequencing:** Does the consultancy stay a nights-and-weekends operation until it proves itself, or is there a trigger point for going full-time?
- **Sustainability:** What's the burnout risk of a split-shift arrangement layered on top of a full-time role?
- **Questions:**
    - What's the honest weekly hour ceiling for each person?
    - Does either current employer need to be informed, or is this strictly outside-hours work?
    - What would have to be true for either person to consider reducing hours at their job?




**3. Pilot Candidate: Open Source / Non-Profit**

Framing: approaching an open-source project or non-profit as a **first pilot**, not a committed client. Several candidates could be considered; Velero is one possibility given existing familiarity with its development community.

- **Why this route:** Open-source teams are already distributed and async-native, so the time-zone gap is a non-issue. They typically lack funded documentation resources. A successful pilot produces a public case study and a testimonial.
- **Candidate criteria:** Active project, real documentation pain, receptive maintainers, visible enough that the case study carries weight.
- **Possible candidates:** Velero (existing contact), plus other infrastructure or developer-tooling projects with known doc gaps.
- **The ask:** Free or low-cost proof of concept in exchange for a case study, testimonial, and possibly an introduction to corporate sponsors who fund such work.
- **Questions:**
    - Which project offers the best combination of access, need, and visibility?
    - What's the smallest scope that still demonstrates the full workflow (reformat → improve → AI-ready → consolidate)?
    - Do we approach one project or prepare a shortlist and pitch two or three?
    - What's the ask — testimonial only, or also a small grant?




**4. Positions and Responsibilities**

- **Responsibilities**: What are the responsibilities
- **Questions**
    - Who is responsible for which parts of the work?
    - Who has the final say on decisions?

5. **Legal Structure & Financial Flow**

- **Entity options:** US LLC (simpler for US clients), Irish Ltd (simpler for the Irish & EU side), or informal partnership to start.
- **Cross-border mechanics:** Payment routing (Wise, Stripe), currency handling (USD/EUR), invoicing and VAT treatment for B2B services.
- **Revenue split:** Equal partners, or weighted by contribution? Decided now or later?
- **Liability:** What exposure exists if a pilot goes wrong while both parties are still employed elsewhere?
- **Questions:**
    - Which entity minimises admin overhead while keeping client trust?
    - Is it worth registering anything before the first pilot, or only once revenue is real?
    - How are profits split, and does that change if one person contributes more hours?




**6. Immediate Next Steps**

- Shortlist pilot candidates and agree on the approach.
- Decide whether any legal entity is needed before the first engagement.
- Fix a recurring meeting slot inside the overlap window.
- Define the first deliverable and who owns it.
