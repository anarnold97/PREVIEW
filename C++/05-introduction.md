# Introduction to C++ (Part 5) 

## Our first project: Print the Times Tables

Write a C++ program that prints the times tables from 1 to 12.

Print one times table per line. Each line should show a number multiplied by 1 through 12.

The first two lines should look like this:
text

```
1 x 1 = 1   1 x 2 = 2   1 x 3 = 3   ...   1 x 12 = 12
2 x 1 = 2   2 x 2 = 4   2 x 3 = 6   ...   2 x 12 = 24
```

Keep going until the last line is the 12 times table.

Your program should:

* Use two nested for loops.
* Use the outer loop to choose which times table to print.
* Use the inner loop to print the 12 equations on that line.
* Move to a new line after each times table.

**Helpful hint:** Multiply the two loop counters to calculate each answer.

### Challenge: 

Add a blank line between each times table to make the output easier to read.

## Interesting fact

Hedy Lamarr was a movie star who also loved inventing things. During World War II, she and composer George Antheil came up with a clever idea for guiding radio-controlled torpedoes: make the radio signal hop between frequencies so it would be harder to jam. Their invention wasn't used during the war, but the idea later helped inspire wireless technology.

Hollywood wanted Hedy to be beautiful and quiet. She had other plans. She later joked that any girl can be glamorous — "all you have to do is stand still and look stupid."

Hedy shows us that you can love more than one thing — movies and machines, music and maths.

Your brain doesn't have to pick just one channel. It can be a whole radio station!
